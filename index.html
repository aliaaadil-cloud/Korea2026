    Before you go

      Where we stay

      Airbnb – Omok-ro, Yangcheon-gu
      All 5 nights, no change. Nearest station: Omokgyo (Line 5). Airport transfers: pre-booked minivan both ways (16 Nov arrival, 21 Nov departure), ₩95,000 per trip, approx. 1 hr by road.

      Good to know

      • Sunset is around 5:15 PM in mid-November – the daylight photo stops (Cafe Pokpo, Haneul Park, Yongmasan, Namsan) are timed for this.
      • Changdeokgung is closed on Mondays; our visit is Tuesday. The Secret Garden can only be visited on a booked guided tour (English: 10:30, 11:30, 2:30, 3:30).
      • Seoul Grand Park zoo closes at 5:00 PM in November (last entry 4:00 PM).
      • Jayeondo salt bread: fresh batches at 9:00, 12:30, 2:00, 3:30, 5:00 and 6:30; minimum order 4 pieces.
      • Naver Map and KakaoMap are more accurate than Google Maps for walking and subway directions in Korea.
      • A transport card (T-money) works on all subways, buses and most taxis.
